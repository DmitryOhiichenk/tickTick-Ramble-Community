Dictate for TickTick

Say your tasks out loud: they appear as cards while you speak and go to your TickTick Inbox in one click.

The app is installed into Applications and launched. Open it with ⌥⇧R (you can change the shortcut in Settings).

After the first launch, open Settings (gear icon) and enter:

  • a Gemini API key — aistudio.google.com → Get API key;
  • a TickTick Open API token — developer.ticktick.com.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Unofficial app, not affiliated with TickTick (Appest Inc.) or Doist. If an older "TickTick Live" is installed, it is replaced; your settings and tokens are kept.
